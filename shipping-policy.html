Last updated: August 21, 2026

At Blegab Luxury Hair, we carefully prepare and package every order to help ensure it arrives safely and in excellent condition.

1. Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 2–5 business days before shipment. Custom or made-to-order wigs may require additional processing time.

2. Shipping Time

Delivery times vary based on your location and the shipping method selected at checkout.

Once your order has shipped, you will receive tracking information when available.

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout unless your order qualifies for a promotional or free-shipping offer displayed on our website.

4. Delivery Delays

Delivery estimates are provided by the shipping carrier and are not guaranteed. Blegab Luxury Hair is not responsible for delays caused by carriers, weather, customs, incorrect delivery information, or circumstances outside our control.

5. International Orders

International customers may be responsible for customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by their country. These fees are not included in the purchase price unless otherwise stated.

6. Incorrect Shipping Information

Please review your shipping address carefully before submitting your order. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible at info@blegab.com.

Once an order has shipped, we may not be able to change the delivery address.
