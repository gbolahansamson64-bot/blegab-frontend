Returns & Refunds Policy

✦

Last updated: August 21, 2026

At Blegab Luxury Hair, customer satisfaction is important to us. Due to the personal nature of human hair products, returns are subject to the conditions below.

1. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:

  • Unworn and unused
  • Unwashed and unaltered
  • In its original condition and packaging
  • Returned with all tags, lace, bands, and packaging intact

Items that have been worn, cut, colored, washed, styled, chemically treated, or otherwise altered cannot be returned.

2. Return Request

If you wish to request a return, please contact us at info@blegab.com within 7 days of delivery.

Please include your order number and reason for the return. Returns should not be sent without prior approval.

3. Custom Orders

Custom-made, personalized, or specially altered wigs are generally not eligible for return or exchange unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order upon delivery.

If you receive an incorrect or damaged item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging so we can review the issue.

5. Refunds

Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to an error by Blegab Luxury Hair.

Please allow additional time for your bank or payment provider to process the refund.

6. Return Shipping

Unless the item is damaged, defective, or incorrect due to our error, customers are responsible for return shipping costs.
